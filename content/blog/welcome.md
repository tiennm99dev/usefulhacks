Every tip on Useful Hacks is now available in Vietnamese. Use the language
switcher at the bottom of the sidebar to change language.

<!--more-->

New in this update:

- **Tags** group related tips across categories, such as all tips for Android.
- **Glossary** explains terms like 2FA or latency; hover an underlined term in
  a tip to see its meaning.
- **Comments** under each tip, through GitHub Discussions.
