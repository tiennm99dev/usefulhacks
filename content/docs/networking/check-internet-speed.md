{{% steps %}}

### Clear the line

Pause other downloads, video and cloud sync on every device.

### Run a test

Open one of these in a browser; each takes under a minute:

- [speed.cloudflare.com](https://speed.cloudflare.com): download, upload,
  {{< term "latency" >}} and {{< term "jitter" >}}.
- [fast.com](https://fast.com): download speed, with more details on request.
- [speedtest.net](https://www.speedtest.net): lets you choose the test server.

### Compare Wi-Fi with cable

Test once over Wi-Fi near the router and once by cable if you can. A big gap
between the two points at the Wi-Fi, not your internet plan.
