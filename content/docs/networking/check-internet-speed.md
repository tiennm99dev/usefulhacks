---
title: Check your internet speed
---

Open one of these in a browser; each test takes under a minute:

- [speed.cloudflare.com](https://speed.cloudflare.com): download, upload,
  latency and jitter.
- [fast.com](https://fast.com): download speed, with more details on request.
- [speedtest.net](https://www.speedtest.net): lets you choose the test server.

For a fair result, pause other downloads and video, and test once over Wi-Fi
near the router and once by cable if you can. A big gap between the two points
at the Wi-Fi, not your internet plan.
