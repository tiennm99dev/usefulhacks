---
title: Reopen a tab you closed by mistake
tags: [shortcut, chrome, edge, firefox]
---

{{< badge content="All major browsers" color="green" >}}

One shortcut brings back the last closed tab, with its history, so Back still
works.

{{< tabs >}}
  {{< tab name="Windows / Linux" >}}Press **Ctrl + Shift + T**.{{< /tab >}}
  {{< tab name="macOS" >}}Press **Cmd + Shift + T**.{{< /tab >}}
{{< /tabs >}}

Press it again to reopen the tab closed before that, and so on. If you closed a
whole window, the same shortcut brings back the window with all its tabs.

> [!TIP]- Closed it a while ago?
> Open the browser's history and look under "Recently closed". The shortcut is
> **Ctrl + H** on Windows and Linux, and on macOS **Cmd + Y** in Chrome, Edge
> and Safari or **Cmd + Shift + H** in Firefox.
