---
title: Reopen a tab you closed by mistake
---

Press **Ctrl + Shift + T** on Windows and Linux, or **Cmd + Shift + T** on
macOS. The last closed tab comes back with its history, so Back still works.

Press it again to reopen the tab closed before that, and so on. If you closed a
whole window, the same shortcut brings back the window with all its tabs.

It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and most other browsers.
