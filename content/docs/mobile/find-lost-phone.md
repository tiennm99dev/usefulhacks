---
title: Find a lost phone
---

Both phone systems can ring, show on a map, lock or erase a lost phone from any
browser, as long as the feature was on before the phone went missing.

- **Android:** open [google.com/android/find](https://www.google.com/android/find)
  and sign in with the Google account on the phone.
- **iPhone:** open [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find) and sign in
  with your Apple Account.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Check now that it is on: on Android, **Settings → Google → Find Hub**; on
iPhone, **Settings → your name → Find My**.
{{< /callout >}}
