---
title: Find a lost phone
tags: [android, iphone, security]
---

Both phone systems can ring, show on a map, lock or erase a lost phone from any
browser, as long as the feature was on before the phone went missing.

{{< tabs >}}
  {{< tab name="Android" >}}
Open [google.com/android/find](https://www.google.com/android/find) and sign in
with the Google account on the phone.

Check that it is on now: **Settings → Google → Find Hub**.
  {{< /tab >}}
  {{< tab name="iPhone" >}}
Open [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find) and sign in with your Apple
Account.

Check that it is on now: **Settings → your name → Find My**.
  {{< /tab >}}
{{< /tabs >}}

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Erase the phone only once you are sure it is gone: after erasing, you can no
> longer locate it.
