---
title: Search smarter with operators
---

Add these to a Google or Bing search to narrow the results:

| Type this | To get |
| --- | --- |
| `"exact phrase"` | Pages with those words in that order |
| `site:example.com tips` | Results from one site only |
| `jaguar -car` | Results without the word after the minus |
| `report filetype:pdf` | Only PDF files (also `docx`, `xlsx`, `pptx`) |
| `intitle:review` | Pages with the word in their title |

Combine them: `site:reddit.com "battery drain" -iphone`.
