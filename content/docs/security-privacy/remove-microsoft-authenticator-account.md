---
title: Remove an account from Microsoft Authenticator on Android
tags: [android, account, security]
---

Delete an old account from Microsoft Authenticator, including a work or school
account that the app refuses to remove.

{{% steps %}}

### Open the account

In Microsoft Authenticator, tap the account you want to delete. Its one-time
code opens.

### Open its settings

Tap the **gear icon** in the top-right corner. On some phones it shows as
**three vertical dots** instead.

### Remove it

Tap **Remove account**, then **Remove** to confirm. The account disappears from
the list.

{{% /steps %}}

> [!NOTE]
> If the app had backed the account up to your Microsoft account, it does not
> come back on this phone unless you sign in to it again.

## When a work or school account cannot be removed

A work or school account is often registered with Android itself, so the
phone's security policies apply to the whole device. Authenticator then blocks
the removal until you take the account off the phone first.

{{% steps %}}

### Open the phone's account list

Open the **Settings** app and go to **Accounts**. On a Samsung phone it is
**Accounts and backup → Manage accounts**.

### Remove the work or school account

Find the email address that is stuck in Authenticator, usually shown with a
Microsoft or Office icon. Tap it, tap **Remove account**, and confirm.

### Remove it from Authenticator again

Go back to Microsoft Authenticator and remove the account as above. It now goes
without the warning.

{{% /steps %}}
