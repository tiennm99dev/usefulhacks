---
title: Turn on two-factor authentication
tags: [account, security]
---

{{< term "2FA" >}} asks for a second proof after your password, so a leaked
password alone is not enough to get into your account.

{{% steps %}}

### Start with the important accounts

Your main email and your phone account first: they can reset every other
password.

### Pick the strongest option offered

1. **Passkey or security key.** A {{< term "passkey" >}} only works on the real
   site, so phishing cannot steal it.
2. **Authenticator app**, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft
   Authenticator or the one built into your password manager.
3. **SMS code.** Better than nothing.

### Save the backup codes

The site shows them once when you turn 2FA on. Store them in your password
manager or print them: they are the way back in if you lose your phone.

{{% /steps %}}

> [!WARNING]- Why is SMS last?
> In a {{< term "SIM swap" >}}, a scammer gets your number moved to their SIM
> and receives your codes. Apps and passkeys are not tied to your number.
