---
title: Turn on two-factor authentication
---

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a second proof after your password, so
a leaked password alone is not enough to get into your account.

Pick the strongest option the site offers, in this order:

1. **Passkey or security key.** Phishing-resistant: it only works on the real
   site.
2. **Authenticator app**, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft
   Authenticator or the one built into your password manager.
3. **SMS code.** Better than nothing, but a SIM swap can steal it.

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
Save the backup codes the site shows when you turn 2FA on. They are the way
back in if you lose your phone.
{{< /callout >}}

Start with the accounts that can reset the others: your main email and your
phone account.
